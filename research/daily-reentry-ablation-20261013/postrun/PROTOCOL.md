## Full population reporting and censoring

The compact report retains all three attempts, compared with both fixed fine controls. It separates full paid-flat, full marked with exposure/not-evaluable, partial/halted, recoverable-but-interrupted, unstarted, and unrecoverable cases. Paid cash deltas require both full paid-flat wallets. Equal full-calendar marked deltas retain residue warnings. Unequal or partial horizons use a common observed completed-minute prefix, including fees, execution costs, funding, turnover, minute-close DD, actual signed/gross exposure and target-weight tracking; a post-last-minute halt mark is excluded from that prefix. No annual return is inferred from a halted path.

Reentry permissions and actual executed OPEN entries are distinct. Entry evidence requires same symbol/signal clock, an OPEN leg from quantity_before zero with sign-consistent nonzero quantity_after, and event time strictly after signal. Subsequent protection cycles are bounded by the next reentry permission for that symbol. Actual terminal observations count the frozen scheduler's eligible attempt-counter increments by order kind; these are scheduler-counted attempt minutes/counters, not accepted fill legs. A due zero-delta order may increment the counter and be marked reached without execute_fill. An ongoing PROTECTIVE_STOP is not relabeled TERMINAL, and below-lot deferred minutes are not counted. Fine controls lack that explicit observation journal, so unsupported actual-attempt counts are unavailable rather than invented. Report includes terminal residue, financial-prefix proof, fees/cost/funding/turnover, all-observation DD, actual exposure and target-weight tracking gap. All financial calculations remain in the frozen simulator/account; streaming observer statistics summarize already saved observations.
