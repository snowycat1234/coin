# Fixed saved-target half mix (historical proxy)

This experiment uses the public saved, already risk-controlled STATIC50 (CS+HOLD) and original downscale-only SMA200 targets. The proposed COMBINED target is exactly `0.5 * STATIC50 + 0.5 * SMA200` per UTC day and CORE5 asset. It is **not** re-risk-scaled after mixing. Opposite signed targets cancel to genuine cash. The original 30% asset and 60% gross caps carry through by convexity. With the saved PSD covariance, estimated annual mix risk is at most the half-weighted component risks by the triangle inequality.

`PROTOCOL.json` was frozen before the saved-year targets were generated; ten synthetic tests and the original-source byte checks passed. `targets/` and `TARGETS_MANIFEST.json` were then produced without calling the economic reference. Original sources are four SHA-pinned public-Git NPZ bytes documented in the adjacent restored source `INDEX.json`; execution inputs are the two pinned original owned-price/funding NPZs. No new market data, fit, variant selection, or holdout access occurred.
