# Fixed half of saved CS+HOLD and SMA200: conditional daily proxy

**Outcome.** The single preregistered target `COMBINED = 0.5 × original STATIC50 + 0.5 × original SMA200_10PCT` passes the frozen technical/economic-quality gates in both known development years. It does **not** improve net profit over the stronger saved STATIC50 component. It is a lower-risk, lower-return compromise in this conditional daily proxy, not an alpha or live-deployment finding.

The [frozen protocol](PROTOCOL.json) and seven initial synthetic assertions were written before targets were prepared. The full final prescore suite had 10 passing tests. Exact four original public-Git NPZ target-source bytes, full 365-day UTC calendars and CORE5 order, two original owned-price/funding input hashes, and deterministic saved target replay were verified. The mix is a literal half of each **already risk-controlled** saved target, without new `risk.scale` or leverage. Signed opposing legs net before the hypothetical trade. Original 30% per-asset and 60% gross caps, and the saved PSD-covariance risk triangle, pass each day. No new provider data, fitting, other weight, or protected holdout was used.

Lower gross and volatility do not by themselves show alpha, and this comparison has no requirement that the mix outperform both components.

**Exploratory saved-trace risk normalization.** Annualized mean daily net return divided by realized annual volatility is 0.326/0.753 for COMBINED and 1.127/1.541 for STATIC50 in 2023/2025, respectively. Reusing the *same saved paired bootstrap indices*, with no new RNG or wallet, yields conditional 95% intervals for COMBINED-minus-STATIC50 on this ratio of [−2.105, +0.556] and [−1.964, +0.273]. Both include zero, and point estimates favor STATIC50. This was assessed after scoring, is not a frozen quality gate, and is not a leveraged equal-risk backtest.

**Do not conflate two 50/50 concepts.** Arithmetic mean of two separately funded component-wallet cumulative returns (initial-capital split) is +2.500%/+6.065% in 2023/2025. Compounding each day's 50/50 average of the separate wallet daily returns (descriptive daily-rebalanced paper benchmark) gives +2.328%/+5.747%. The COMBINED results above instead execute the netted half-sum target in one 10,000 USDT hypothetical wallet, with its own quantities, turnover, costs, funding and NAV. These three computations need not agree; no additional shared-wallet trades/costs are charged to the two descriptive benchmark formulas.
