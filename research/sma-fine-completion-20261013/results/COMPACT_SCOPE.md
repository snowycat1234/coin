Unequal observed horizons use a common completed-minute prefix.

All full local source caches, account journals and lossless recovery snapshots/chunks remain retained locally. They are not uploaded by this compact release. The remote compact branch is not a full native-wallet restoration pack. No market provider download, Library access, new 2026 retained-source read, trading or credential operation occurred.

The financial runner/cases/targets/source/profile were frozen, independently reviewed and remote-byte verified before historical launch. The observer was prepared in parallel and its final manifest was frozen after historical launch, before any terminal capture or observer execution; its independent review and hash-bound GO preceded all three actual cold restores and the report. It must not be described as a pre-launch observer freeze.

All results are conditional already-seen development simulation. Fine 1e-8 quantity resolution, original RAW_AS_FRACTION funding, fees, source arrival and cross-venue execution assumptions are not venue certification, out-of-sample evidence or a future-performance promise. No strategy or parameter was chosen by these outcomes.
