## Unchanged model and exact preprocessing/loss

Use only raw training_fixture.json x values. Ignore its full-506 scaler and z values. For each fold separately, let N be its prefix date count and n_d its number of supervised noncash experts. Each observation gets weight 1/(N*n_d). Compute a single shared mean and population variance for each of the ten features using these weights. CASH is excluded. Zero-variance columns are mapped to zero in training and forecast; otherwise z=(x-mu)/sqrt(variance). No later date, unavailable expert or evaluation state contributes to scaling.

Exactly 15 parameters: shared ten-vector w and five noncash biases b_e. For available noncash e, s_de=w dot z_de+b_e. CASH score is exactly zero. Loss is sum over dates and supervised noncash experts of 0.5*(s_de-u_de)^2/(N*n_d), plus 0.5*(sum(w^2)+sum(b^2)); lambda is fixed at 1.0. Native source utility, no target normalization, no feature search, no penalty search, no threshold tuning. Missing vectors stay null and masked, never zero-filled. Training-label maturity is separate from decision-time availability. At forecast choose max score using causal availability only; CASH wins a tie with CASH, otherwise original expert order. Noncash must have strictly positive maximum score.

If authorized, use precisely the original frozen quadratic linear solve, two separate fold-specific designs and scalers, with finite solution/objective and gradient infinity norm <=1e-8. No optimizer fallback or changed lambda. Run synthetic structural tests first. Source-preserved utility semantics and original feature definitions remain in FROZEN_PROTOCOL.md. Scores are not certified or calibrated risk/profitability estimates, and cannot be used for wallet allocations.
