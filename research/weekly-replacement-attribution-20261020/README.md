# Fixed weekly membership replacement attribution

This is a new, predeclared mechanism diagnostic on the restored public 2023 CORE5 source, not a new trading strategy, a shared-wallet replay, or recovery of an interrupted account. It asks whether the original weekly CS raw LONG membership replacements produced enough incremental next-week price and funding value to pay their additional switching costs.

The original 21-return ranking, 31-close eligibility, stable asset-order ties and Monday clock are unchanged. A positive raw target is a LONG sleeve, not a positive absolute-momentum filter. Prices after a decision are used only as outcomes, never to choose members. All scheduled weeks are classified; no favorable month, asset, rank threshold or holding-window selection is allowed.

Two fixed reference slots each carry 0.5 USDT at the decision reference price. Unchanged names cancel. Entrant and displaced sets are compared without pair optimization, padding a smaller set with CASH. Existing incumbent acquisition is sunk. Switching pays a sale of the outgoing asset and purchase of the incoming asset; both alternatives pay their own terminal sale. CASH has no asset price change, funding or fill cost. This deliberately avoids the erroneous cancellation obtained by charging identical fresh round trips to both alternatives.

The holding interval is the original decision plus one minute through the next scheduled decision plus one minute. The final interval crossing beyond the restored 2023 minute source is retained as right-censored, not shortened. Unknown or invalid holdings cannot be silently retained or filled; the specification defines fail-closed treatment. Original warmup source establishes prior membership, never an assumed initial CASH state.

Costs use the previously declared conditional BASE27 schedule: 8 basis points execution adjustment per side plus 5.5 basis points fees on the adjusted fill notional. Funding uses actual event times and raw fractional rates; LONG pays positive rates. The fixed strictly past mark CLOSE proxy remains a proxy, not a certified venue settlement charge mark.

Primary inference uses all complete original weekly intervals, including zero replacement contributions on unchanged weeks. Conditional changed-week summaries are secondary. Fixed joint two-week circular blocks and a fixed seed describe sampling uncertainty on seen development history, not research-selection-adjusted out-of-sample evidence.

The result can characterize this recovered 2023 original signal opportunity. It cannot explain all differences in the inaccessible 2023–2025 rank-retention wallets or establish live profit, identical risk, native fills, minQty/capacity/margin feasibility, or reinvested account NAV. No 2026 holdout, provider download, model training, or lost-account reconstruction is part of this module.

See SPEC.json for exact frozen input identities and conventions, DEDUPLICATION.json for bounded prior-work comparison, analyze.py for the implementation, and generated tests for the accounting and causal boundary checks. Code and specification are published before the actual outcome calculation; resulting artifacts will be added only afterward.
