# Historical OI is feasible for retrospective development, with meaningful limits

Official Binance USD-M daily metrics archives contain usable CORE5 open-interest observations through April 2024. **45 representative ZIPs and their official SHA256 sidecars verified; 28 availability HEAD receipts retained.** No bulk history, feature construction, fit, inference, threshold change, or wallet replay occurred. This establishes sample-level input feasibility, not a predictive edge, full-history completeness, or historical as-of availability.

## Coverage and training-history tradeoff

- **BTCUSDT:** 2020-09-01 is the first directly verified raw sample; the adjacent 2020-08-31 path is 404. January–July 2021 first-day HEAD anchors, 2020-10-15 and 2021-11-30 exist. The 2020-09-01 and 2021-01-01 ZIPs each contain 576 physical rows at 288 timestamps. Every repeated CSV-line byte sequence is identical, with no conflicting values. Exact deduplication would be lossless for these two samples.
- **ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, XRPUSDT, DOGEUSDT:** each is 404 at the checked 2020-10-15, 2021-01-01, 2021-07-01 and 2021-11-30 paths. Each has a complete positive 288-label raw sample on 2021-12-01. This is the first directly verified common CORE5 sample, not a proven absolute beginning or a proof that every intermediate day exists. Archive absence must not be mistaken for contract absence; official launch announcements already place [XRP in January 2020](https://www.binance.com/en-IN/support/announcement/detail/360038497311) and [DOGE in July 2020](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/7777402b13574c41afe13b334d3e047e).
- All five have checked ZIPs on 2021-12-01, 2022-01-01, 2022-12-31, 2023-01-01, 2023-12-31 and 2024-04-30. The first five anchors are complete positive chronological 288-point grids. All five April 30 samples are complete positive unique grids after timestamp sorting; physical CSV order is nonchronological.

If complete daily coverage begins at the common December 2021 anchor, the proposed D+2-midnight availability proxy plus the existing 64-day window would first yield a fully observed OI-level window on **2022-02-04**. Only **310 of the existing 744 mature TRAIN decision dates** are on or after that date, before further gaps. A 64-day window of one-day OI changes starts 2022-02-05, giving 309 dates. These are conditional calendar upper bounds for **fully observed OI windows**, not certified continuous coverage or independent observations. Existing price-history training dates need not be discarded: an explicitly versioned future adapter can retain 744 dates and carry OI missing masks separately. Informative OI history remains shorter. A matched-window no-OI comparison would be needed to separate extra-input effects from training-window changes; none was run.

## Actual schema, stocks and units

Every sampled CSV has exactly eight fields: create_time, symbol, sum_open_interest, sum_open_interest_value, count_toptrader_long_short_ratio, sum_toptrader_long_short_ratio, count_long_short_ratio and sum_taker_long_short_vol_ratio. Archive labels are timezone-naive date-time strings, normally on a five-minute grid. UTC interpretation is an explicit diagnostic convention, not a reconstructed publication clock.

Current [official API documentation](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-derivatives-trading-usd-s-m-futures/api/rest-api/market-data) identifies aggregate OI and OI value, limits REST history to the latest month, and describes its numeric timestamp as the period's end in milliseconds. It does not map the archive's create_time to those semantics retrospectively. No trading REST request was made.

Quantity/value fields are consistent with base-asset quantity and USDT notional, but native historical units and the valuation-price formula remain uncertified. In December 1 samples, median value/quantity ratios are BTC 57183.945, ETH 4704.6019, SOL 222.2135, XRP 1.0047312 and DOGE 0.214570925. This is a unit-consistency inference, not an independent price-source verification. [Official contract specifications](https://www.binance.com/en-AE/support/faq/detail/360033161972) describe linear USDT/USDC contracts and base-asset contract units, including BTC/ETH examples; the page was updated in 2026 and does not certify this archive's historical conversions. Cross-asset raw quantities must not be summed. Notional change also contains price movement. OI is an outstanding-position stock; summing 288 snapshots is not daily new positioning or volume.

## Quality and feasible daily treatment

Offline recomputation recovers **32/45 complete positive unique grids** using only timestamp sort and byte-identical dedup. **37/45** have a positive unambiguous 23:55 labelled terminal observation. Original ZIPs remain immutable; these are normalization diagnostics, not adopted features.

- All five March 7, 2022 samples have 102 invalid OI rows from 15:30 through 23:55. Their daily terminal observation is invalid.
- All five March 8 samples have 15 invalid OI rows, including the first 14 five-minute labels and a 07:10 anomaly. Their 23:55 observation is valid, but a daily average requiring the whole grid is invalid. A March 8 one-day terminal change is also invalid because March 7's terminal is missing.
- BTC February 16, 2024 stops at 13:30: 163 rows, 125 missing labels, no terminal. A [Binance collaborator confirms the missing records cannot be restored](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/issues/335#issuecomment-6020310934).
- SOL December 8/12, 2023 has 280/279 rows, 8/9 missing labels, with last labels 23:20/23:15. No daily terminal can be inferred from the earlier final row.

A future daily-stock contract can select the actual 23:55 labelled row after sorting and lossless identical dedup; an absent, nonpositive, nonfinite or conflicting endpoint stays UNKNOWN. A full-day mean needs all required valid grid labels. Each change needs both valid endpoints, and every sequence keeps its missing mask. Do not substitute the physical last row, forward-fill outages, or turn missing/zero archive data into a zero change. These are proposed rules only.

## Observation time is not availability

The [official public-data README](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/f446ce3812bd4e5521f21faecd4ae3c6460e49fc/README.md) says daily files normally become available the next day and archives can later be revised. It does not provide a precise historical intraday release clock. Current HTTP Last-Modified metadata for checked old BTC objects points to March 18, 2026; it cannot certify what a 2021 model could download. A [Binance collaborator's February 2023 statement](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/issues/211#issuecomment-1430648564) planned finer OI granularity for later in 2023. Identical current intraday 2021/2022 versions must not be presumed contemporaneously public. An [unanswered provenance request](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/issues/509) is only a third-party question, not Binance confirmation.

**Conservative retrospective proposal:** for a midnight UTC decision T, use archive day D only when D<=T−2 calendar days. Keep the original observation label, assumed availability proxy and UNKNOWN original publication separate. This avoids assuming a next-day archive is already present at that next day's 00:00, but cannot certify first release or exclude later backfills/revisions. Any later study must be labelled current-vintage retrospective development, with 2023 already selected and 2024 already seen. No as-of, pristine OOS, or live-return claim follows.

## Decision and reproducibility

Proceedable as a clearly labelled retrospective input source with explicit missing masks. Full-date ingestion/completeness is a separate bounded step before training. BTC-only older context and common CORE5 inputs have different histories; do not silently substitute the former for the latter. No evidence here establishes OI forecast skill at the project's 21-day horizon or improves the frozen selector.
