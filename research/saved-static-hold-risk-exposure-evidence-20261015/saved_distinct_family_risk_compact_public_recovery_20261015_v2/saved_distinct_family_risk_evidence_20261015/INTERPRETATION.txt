Saved fixed-family development evidence: what the comparison does and does not show

All six matched year/tier raw paid annual returns are higher for
VOL_MANAGED_HOLD than STATIC50 in these saved adaptive2023/2024 observations.
The raw annual-return gaps (HOLD minus STATIC) are:
-2023 LOW +5.140138 percentage points; MID +0.451120; HIGH +10.824308.
-2024 LOW +0.805376 percentage points; MID +8.426232; HIGH +21.042247.

This is not equal-risk evidence. HOLD's realized daily annualized volatility is
higher in all six pairs. Its native all-observation MDD is higher in four pairs;
it is lower for2023 LOW and2024 HIGH. The annual return divided by full-sample
realized volatility is higher for HOLD in three pairs and STATIC in three.
These future-informed descriptive ratios cannot establish an executable scaled
strategy, out-of-sample performance, risk-matched alpha or superiority.

Exposure composition matters. HOLD mean gross exposure is lower in five of six
pairs (the exception is2023 HIGH), while HOLD mean signed net exposure is higher
in every pair. HOLD remains long-only in the saved signed-weight telemetry;
STATIC includes both signs. Equal nominal volatility budgets do not equate
actual gross, directional market exposure, realized volatility or drawdowns.

HOLD has lower paid turnover, fees and execution-price costs in all six pairs.
Its signed funding is more negative in every pair. All funding signs are kept:
positive is a receipt; negative is a payment. More paid trade legs do not imply
more notional turnover. The included same-executed-quantity PnL bridge is an
accounting decomposition, not an independently executable no-cost strategy.

All six HOLD cases have zero initial Guard50 paid-warning signals. STATIC has
2/0/3 signals at2023 LOW/MID/HIGH and2/3/4 at2024 LOW/MID/HIGH. The six H090
paths are byte-proven exact STATIC financial/minute duplicates, despite some
different targets/requests, and provide no additional independent confirmation.
Forecast volatility overshoot minutes are higher for HOLD in every matched pair;
these are approved strictly-past frozen-covariance model diagnostics, not true
exchange risk or a continuous-cap certification.

Every year is a separate fresh10000-USDT annual account. No annual returns are
multiplied into a two-year equity curve or CAGR. No partial baseline prefix is
used as a full-year comparator. The analysis does not fetch raw/new/holdout data,
replay accounts, retune thresholds, construct scaled trades or implement a causal
normalization policy.

Saved minute NAV returns are deterministic float64 telemetry, not an exact
native40 ledger. Native40 chronological proofs remain authoritative and raw100
captured-delta residuals stay visible. Native all-observation MDD, minute-close
MDD and daily-close MDD retain separate scopes. The2024 original carry-forward/
unknown causal-mark-quality sidecar remains bound; all fixed calendar minutes
are retained without outcome/quality filtering. Native venue execution, marks,
liquidation/insurance, filters and funding-publication assumptions are uncertified.

The compact lossless daily records reproduce the module's daily curves, exposure
aggregates and paired tables. Minute-return hashes and all-minute extrema cannot
be independently deep-recomputed without the omitted original hash-pinned
MINUTE.npz archives. No full minute archive is included in this small module.
