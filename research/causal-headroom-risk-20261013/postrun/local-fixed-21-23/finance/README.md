Each year includes byte-identical final and last preterminal day360 complete wallet snapshots, final POINTER, original RESULT/BINDING/DAILY/numeric hashes and an XZ-compressed original simulated ACCOUNT journal. ACCOUNT.json.xz decompresses with Python lzma or xz to the exact original raw SHA256 and byte count in the manifest. Original complete local durable state and all365 per-year minute chunks remain preserved and were independently cold-checked with zero financial advances. Export preparation itself imports no simulator and advances no wallet.

The730 referenced DAY chunks are deliberately not duplicated here. This public finance package therefore supports recovery of the final financial snapshot/account and day360 financial checkpoint, but it is not a standalone full-minute cold-audit archive. A full-minute cold audit still needs the hash-bound original DAY chunks and supplied source/feature/runtime dependencies. Do not interpret POINTER references as files included in this package. The day360 checkpoints are retained as existing evidence; no checkpoint was branched or advanced for another experiment.
